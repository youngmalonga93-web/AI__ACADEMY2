## Attribution Policy

- Every lesson page shows an "Approved YouTube Teachings" section when third-party videos are recommended.
- Every lesson page shows "Third-Party Shout Outs" so learners know these creators deserve credit.
- AI Academy should eventually produce original video lessons and keep third-party links as supplemental references.
- If a creator asks us to remove or update a link, we should comply quickly.

## Content Safety Notes

- Prefer official channels, universities, major labs, and respected educators.
- Avoid hype-only videos, unverifiable income claims, and channels that overpromise outcomes.
- Review external links quarterly because video availability, titles, and channel policies can change.
